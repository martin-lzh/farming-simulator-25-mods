# Community guidelines / 社区规范

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

Treat players and contributors with respect. Discuss ideas and behavior rather than making personal attacks. Welcome different experience levels, give actionable feedback, and respect people's language, identity and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and spam are not acceptable in this project's issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

Report concerns to the maintainer through an existing private channel. If none is available, ask for one without identifying the affected person or posting private evidence in an issue. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, hear relevant context and explain moderation decisions to those affected where appropriate. They may remove content, warn participants or restrict participation according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Concerns involving a maintainer can also be reported through the platform's reporting tools.

## 中文

尊重玩家及贡献者，针对想法和行为讨论，避免人身攻击。欢迎不同经验水平的参与者，提供可执行的反馈，尊重他人的语言、身份与隐私。

本项目的 Issue、PR 及其他交流空间不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视性侮辱、不受欢迎的性关注、泄露私人信息或垃圾信息。

请通过已有私密渠道向维护者反映问题；没有该渠道时，可先请求联系方式，不在 Issue 中透露当事人身份或私人证据。维护者应审慎处理、听取相关背景，并在合适情况下向受影响者说明处理依据；根据行为的严重程度及重复情况，可以移除内容、警告或限制参与。涉及维护者本人的问题也可通过平台举报工具反馈。
