# Changelog / 版本记录

## Unreleased

- First version. Choose what an empty fertilizer spreader, sprayer or lime spreader will apply with Y (Shift+Y goes back), like choosing seeds on a seeder; the empty tank shows your choice.
- 首个版本。像在播种机上选种子一样，按 Y（Shift+Y 反向）为空的撒肥机、喷雾机或石灰撒布机选择要施用的类型；空料箱上会显示所选类型。
- If the implement or combination already uses Y, such as a seeder with several seeds or Precision Farming, the key is Alt+Y.
- 如果机具或组合已经使用 Y（例如可选多种种子的播种机或精准农业），按键改为 Alt+Y。
- AI workers buy the chosen type, lime and herbicide included, when "AI Worker Refill - Fertilizer" is on, and work the parts of the field that need it. Lime-only spreaders get lime without any key.
- 开启“AI 帮手补充 - 肥料”后，AI 帮手会购买所选类型（包括石灰和除草剂），并处理需要它的田地区域。只能装石灰的撒布机无需按键即可购买石灰。
- When the tank holds something, its content is used and the choice is locked until the tank is empty again.
- 料箱里有东西时按实际内容施用，选择被锁定，直到料箱再次清空。
- Works in multiplayer: the choice is synchronized and saved with the vehicle, and only the server pays for bought fill.
- 支持多人游戏：选择会同步并随车辆保存，购买费用只由服务器结算。
- Available in all 27 game languages.
- 支持全部 27 种游戏语言。
