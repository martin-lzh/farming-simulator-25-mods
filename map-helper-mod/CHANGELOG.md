# Changelog / 版本记录

## Unreleased

- First version. Adds a Map Helper drawer to the map page of the in-game menu that works with the vehicle you are sitting in. Open it with the Map Helper button below your balance or with Left Alt + H.
- 首个版本。在游戏菜单的地图页面加入“地图助手”抽屉，直接作用于你当前驾驶的车辆。点击余额下方的“地图助手”按钮或按左 Alt + H 打开。
- Center on me moves the map to your vehicle, or to you when you are on foot.
- “定位到我”将地图移到你的车辆；步行时移到你所在的位置。
- Create job, Set destination, Start job and Cancel job open the game's own worker menus and actions for your vehicle, without searching for it on the map.
- “创建作业”“设置目的地”“开始作业”“取消作业”直接为你的车辆打开游戏自带的帮手菜单和操作，无需在地图上寻找车辆。
- Command queue: in the job menu, Add to queue (Left Alt + J) lines up the job instead of starting it. Start queue runs the jobs one after another; each starts when the previous one is finished.
- 指令队列：在作业菜单中，“加入队列”（左 Alt + J）会把作业排入队列而不立即开始。“开始队列”依次执行作业，每个作业在上一个完成后开始。
- Queued field work uses the worker settings the vehicle has when the job starts.
- 排队的田地工作使用作业开始时车辆的帮手设置。
- If a job is stopped or can't be finished, the queue pauses and you get a message. The drawer shows the waiting jobs, the reason for a pause, and lets you delete single jobs or clear the queue.
- 作业被停止或无法完成时，队列暂停并提示你。抽屉显示等待中的作业和暂停原因，可删除单个作业或清空队列。
- Queues are saved with the game. In multiplayer, the server runs them, farm members allowed to hire workers can change them, and players who join later see them.
- 队列随存档保存。多人游戏中由服务器执行，有雇佣帮手权限的农场成员可以修改，之后加入的玩家也能看到。
- Available in all 27 game languages.
- 支持全部 27 种游戏语言。
