# Changelog / 版本记录

## Unreleased

- First version. Adds a Map Helper page to the left sidebar of the map in the in-game menu, next to the map filters and Active Workers.
- 首个版本。在游戏菜单地图的左侧边栏加入“地图助手”页面，与地图筛选和“在岗帮手”并列。
- Left Alt + M opens the map on the Map Helper page, on foot or in a vehicle. On the map, the Map Helper button below your balance and its key (X by default) switch to the page.
- 左 Alt + M 直接打开地图并显示地图助手页面，步行或驾驶时都可以。在地图上，余额下方的“地图助手”按钮及其按键（默认为 X）切换到该页面。
- The page works with the vehicle you are sitting in. Click another of your vehicles on the map to plan for it; My vehicle takes you back.
- 页面作用于你当前驾驶的车辆。在地图上点击你的其他车辆即可为它安排作业；“我的车辆”回到你自己的车辆。
- Places lists your running contracts, your fields, the jobs the vehicle can do and the stations it can load at or deliver to. Click one to move the map there and fill in the job.
- “地点”列出进行中的合同、你的田地、车辆能执行的作业，以及它可以装货或卸货的站点。点击一项，地图会移到该处并填好作业。
- Jobs are set up with the game's own job settings, including picking the target on the map. Add to queue (Z) puts the job at the end, Insert as next (X) puts it first.
- 作业使用游戏自带的作业设置，包括在地图上选择目标。“加入队列”（Z）把作业排到最后，“插入为下一个”（X）把作业排到最前。
- The command queue works like a to-do list: the current job shows what it is doing, the distance to its target and the progress of a contract, finished jobs disappear and the next one starts by itself.
- 指令队列就像待办清单：当前作业显示正在做什么、离目标的距离和合同进度，完成的作业自动消失，下一个自动开始。
- Waiting jobs can be moved up or down, edited and deleted at any time, also while a worker is driving. The queue then continues after the current job, even one you started yourself.
- 等待中的作业随时可以上移、下移、编辑和删除，帮手驾驶时也可以。队列会在当前作业之后继续，即使这个作业是你直接开始的。
- Queued field work uses the worker settings the vehicle has when the job starts.
- 排队的田地工作使用作业开始时车辆的帮手设置。
- If a job is stopped or can't be finished, the queue pauses, shows the reason and you get a message. Start queue continues it.
- 作业被停止或无法完成时，队列暂停并显示原因，同时提示你。点击“开始队列”继续。
- Queues are saved with the game. In multiplayer, the server runs them, farm members allowed to hire workers can change them, and players who join later see them.
- 队列随存档保存。多人游戏中由服务器执行，有雇佣帮手权限的农场成员可以修改，之后加入的玩家也能看到。
- Available in all 27 game languages.
- 支持全部 27 种游戏语言。
