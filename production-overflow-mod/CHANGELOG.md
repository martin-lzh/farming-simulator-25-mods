# Changelog / 版本记录

## Unreleased

- First version. Adds two output modes to the production menu, after Distributing: "Distributing, store rest automatically" and "Distributing, sell rest automatically". Choose them with Change output mode, per production point and product.
- 首个版本。在生产菜单的“分配”之后新增两种输出模式：“分配，剩余自动储存”和“分配，剩余自动出售”。用“更改输出模式”为每个生产点的每种产品选择。
- Both deliver to your own production points first, once per in-game hour, exactly like Distributing.
- 两者都与“分配”完全一样，每游戏小时先送往自己农场的生产点。
- Distributing, store rest automatically: what doesn't fit becomes full pallets, as in Storing. A smaller rest, and products made during the hour, wait in the factory for the next delivery. Only offered for products that come out on pallets.
- 分配，剩余自动储存：放不下的部分按“储存”生成满托盘。不足一个托盘的余量和这一小时内的新产出留在工厂，等待下一次分配。只对以托盘产出的产品提供。
- Distributing, sell rest automatically: what doesn't fit is sold at the same price as Selling.
- 分配，剩余自动出售：放不下的部分按“出售”的价格卖出。
- The product list on the Production Chains page shows the new modes by name.
- “生产链”页面的产品列表会显示新模式的名称。
- Works in multiplayer: farm members allowed to manage production points can change the modes, players who join later receive them, and only the server sells and makes pallets.
- 支持多人游戏：有“管理生产点”权限的农场成员可以修改模式，之后加入的玩家也会同步，出售和生成托盘只由服务器处理。
- Without the Mod, outputs set to a new mode use Distributing.
- 卸载 Mod 后，设为新模式的产出改为“分配”。
- Available in all 27 game languages.
- 支持全部 27 种游戏语言。
